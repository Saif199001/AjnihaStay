## Scope

Recurring billing orchestration has one canonical production service boundary in `payments/recurring_billing_service.py`.

The canonical service owns:

- workspace-scoped billing schedule lookup;
- row locking with `select_for_update()`;
- active schedule and occupancy validation;
- exact `next_run_date` enforcement;
- recurring invoice/charge orchestration;
- atomic transaction boundaries;
- schedule cursor advancement; and
- recurring invoice ledger posting.

## Authority Boundary

`tenant.charge_service.create_charge()` explicitly rejects schedule-linked charges. Schedule-linked recurring charges must be generated through the canonical recurring billing service.

The existing schedule and invoice entry points are compatibility facades that delegate to the canonical service rather than maintaining independent orchestration logic.

## Verified Guarantees

- Workspace isolation is enforced for recurring schedule lookup.
- Concurrent schedule execution is serialized by row locking.
- Invoice creation, charge creation, and schedule advancement are atomic.
- Existing recurring invoice retries are idempotent.
- A schedule-linked charge cannot bypass the canonical recurring billing authority through the public charge service.
- Regression tests cover monthly and daily generation, exact next-run enforcement, workspace isolation, inactive schedules, direct schedule-link rejection, atomic rollback, idempotent retry, and duplicate schedule advancement prevention.
