# P1.3 — Lease Service Hardening & Contract Verification

Status: HARDENING APPLIED — CI GATE PENDING

## Contract

P1.3 is the canonical service boundary for Lease mutations. It sits above the existing Lease model and Occupancy relationship and does not replace Occupancy semantics or the financial core.

## Verified invariants

- Lease mutations require active workspace Owner/Admin/Manager permission.
- Lease creation is workspace-scoped and locks the target Occupancy before mutation.
- Occupancy must belong to the requested workspace.
- New leases always begin in `draft` status.
- Unsupported fields are rejected rather than silently ignored.
- Update operations cannot mutate workspace, occupancy, creator, lifecycle status, or lifecycle audit timestamps.
- Contractual date and monetary validation is applied at the service boundary.
- Money is non-negative and limited to two decimal places.
- Lifecycle transitions are explicit and atomic.
- Same-status lifecycle requests are idempotent no-ops.
- Terminal states cannot transition onward.
- Lifecycle actor is recorded through `updated_by`.
- Activation, termination, and cancellation timestamps are assigned by the canonical transition service.
- Existing Lease→Occupancy OneToOne semantics remain unchanged. Renewal/version history is deferred to P1.6 and must not be implemented by silently changing this relationship.
- P1.3 does not create invoices, payments, charges, allocations, or other financial-core records.
- Existing P0.1–P0.12 contracts remain outside the P1.3 mutation boundary.

## Database contract

P1.2 database constraints, explicit short index names, workspace RLS/Force RLS, and OneToOne occupancy protection remain authoritative. P1.3 adds service-level protection and regression coverage without replacing those database guarantees.

## Verification scope

Dedicated regression tests cover authorization, workspace isolation, field allowlisting, date/money validation, lifecycle transition validity, terminal-state protection, idempotent transitions, database uniqueness, and the financial boundary.

## Lock rule

P1.3 may be marked HARDENED/CONTRACT LOCKED only after the exact latest commit passes the repository CI migration graph, migrations, RLS, full Django test suite, and system checks.
