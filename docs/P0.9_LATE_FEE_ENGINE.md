# P0.9 — Late Fee Engine

**Status:** Architecture LOCKED

## Purpose

Late fees are receivable-side financial facts derived from an invoice becoming overdue. They must not mutate historical payments, advance credit, or existing adjustment semantics.

## Locked financial semantics

1. An invoice becomes overdue only when `as_of > due_date` and there is still collectible outstanding balance.
2. The collectible balance is the canonical invoice financial position after existing debit/reducing adjustments and payment/advance-credit settlement.
3. A late fee increases receivable; it does not represent cash settlement.
4. Existing payment allocations, advance-credit applications, and historical payment amounts remain unchanged.
5. Late-fee calculation must be deterministic for the same invoice, policy, and `as_of` date.
6. Late fees must never compound on previously generated late fees unless a future explicitly approved policy says so.
7. A late fee must not be generated when the invoice is fully settled as of the calculation date.
8. Future-dated calculations are allowed only when explicitly supplied by a caller; default execution uses the current business date supplied by the service boundary.

## Policy model

The engine will support a workspace-scoped late-fee policy with:

- enabled/disabled state
- grace period in calendar days after the invoice due date
- calculation mode: fixed amount or percentage
- configured rate/amount with two-decimal monetary precision
- minimum overdue balance threshold
- optional maximum late fee cap

The initial implementation is intentionally invoice-scoped. No tenant-wide wallet or global balance mutation is introduced in P0.9.

## Calculation boundary

For an invoice with due date `D` and calculation date `A`:

`overdue_start = D + grace_days + 1 day`

No late fee is eligible when `A < overdue_start` or canonical collectible outstanding is `<= 0`.

For fixed mode:

`fee = configured_fixed_amount`

For percentage mode:

`fee = collectible_outstanding * configured_percentage / 100`

The result is rounded to two decimal places and capped by the configured maximum when present.

## Idempotency

Generation is idempotent per invoice + policy + effective overdue period. Replaying the same generation request must return the existing late-fee fact rather than create another charge.

A late-fee record must be immutable after creation. Corrections will use a future explicit reversal/adjustment mechanism, never in-place financial mutation.

## Canonical transition

P0.9 introduces a `GENERATE_LATE_FEE` financial transition. The transition delegates to a transactional late-fee service which:

1. authorizes the workspace mutation;
2. locks the workspace/invoice as required;
3. validates workspace ownership and invoice eligibility;
4. calculates the deterministic fee;
5. creates one immutable late-fee financial fact under an idempotency constraint;
6. recomputes the canonical invoice position;
7. updates invoice derived paid/status fields without changing settlement facts.

## Concurrency

Concurrent generation for the same invoice/policy/period must serialize through the invoice/workspace locking strategy and database uniqueness. A race must result in one financial fact and one deterministic replay.

## Protected contracts

P0.9 does not change:

- invoice statuses (`pending`, `partial`, `paid`)
- payment and payment-allocation semantics
- advance-credit semantics
- adjustment semantics
- recurring billing idempotency
- workspace isolation/RLS
- RBAC authorization
- occupancy semantics
- existing API contracts

## Future extensions

Explicitly deferred: recurring late-fee schedules, tenant-wide policies, tax/GST accounting treatment, credit-note/refund reversal workflow, accounting/GL postings, waiver approval workflow, notification automation, and reporting dashboards.
