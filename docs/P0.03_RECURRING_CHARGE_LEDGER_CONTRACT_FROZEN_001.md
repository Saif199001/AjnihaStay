## Scope

P0-03 closes the recurring-charge ledger posting contract. Recurring charge and recurring invoice orchestration remain owned by the canonical recurring billing service, while ledger posting occurs only after the required financial records and relationships exist.

## Frozen Contract

1. `payments/recurring_billing_service.py` is the canonical recurring-charge and recurring-invoice orchestration boundary.
2. Schedule-linked direct calls through `tenant.charge_service.create_charge()` are rejected.
3. Recurring charge creation uses the lower-level charge persistence helper with `post_ledger=False`, then posts the canonical `charge_generated` ledger event from the recurring orchestration layer.
4. Recurring invoice generation creates the invoice and charge atomically, posts the invoice-linked `charge_generated` event, advances the schedule, and posts `recurring_invoice_generated`.
5. Replaying an already-created recurring charge is read-only/idempotent and does not attempt to reconstruct or repost a ledger event without reliable invoice context.
6. Ledger event identity and immutability remain enforced by `payments.ledger_service.post_ledger_event()`; conflicting reuse of an event key is rejected.
7. Schedule locking, workspace scoping, exact `next_run_date` validation, and atomic schedule advancement remain part of the canonical orchestration contract.

## Known Non-Blocking Reconciliation Concern

The `post_ledger=False` control is intentionally used by canonical orchestration when it must create the financial record before posting the final ledger event. A future caller must not use this mode without owning the corresponding canonical ledger posting. Reconciliation of any legacy/inconsistent charge-without-ledger state is outside P0-03 and must not weaken ledger immutability.

## Freeze Rule

This checkpoint is the production baseline for P0-03. Future changes to recurring-charge ledger timing, event identity, replay behavior, schedule advancement, or the direct-charge boundary require a new audit and a new numbered freeze checkpoint; this frozen contract must not be silently altered.
