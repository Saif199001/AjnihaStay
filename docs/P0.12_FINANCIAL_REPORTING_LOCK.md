# P0.12 — Financial Reporting

**Status: COMPLETE — LOCKED**

## Final Decision

P0.12 Financial Reporting is complete as a read-only reporting and reconciliation projection layer over the canonical financial state and immutable financial ledger established in P0.1–P0.11.

P0.12 introduces no competing financial authority and does not rewrite or alter prior financial semantics.

## Completed Workstreams

### P0.12-A — Reporting Architecture

- Locked the reporting source-of-truth boundary.
- Canonical financial state remains authoritative for current derived balances and lifecycle.
- Immutable ledger remains authoritative for historical financial events.
- Reporting is read-only and workspace/RLS protected.
- Temporal, Decimal, rebuildability, and reconciliation boundaries are explicit.

### P0.12-B — Reporting Query Services

Implemented read-only reporting services for:

- invoice financial position;
- receivables;
- invoice status;
- workspace collections;
- advance/prepaid credit;
- adjustments;
- late fees;
- ledger activity.

### P0.12-C — Period and Aging Reports

Implemented deterministic period and aging projections:

- inclusive collection period boundaries;
- payment-date semantics for recorded payments;
- successful-refund event-date semantics;
- explicit `as_of` aging date;
- `current`, `1_30`, `31_60`, `61_90`, and `90_plus` buckets;
- future-due invoices remain current;
- current canonical financial position is used without pretending to reconstruct historical balances.

### P0.12-D — Ledger Reconciliation

Implemented a read-only deterministic reconciliation report covering:

- missing expected events;
- unexpected events;
- orphan/source anomalies;
- amount mismatches;
- relationship mismatches.

Reconciliation never performs automatic repair.

### P0.12-E — Reporting API

Exposed authenticated, workspace-scoped GET endpoints for the complete P0.12 reporting surface:

- `/api/reports/receivables/`
- `/api/reports/invoice/`
- `/api/reports/invoice-status/`
- `/api/reports/collections/period/`
- `/api/reports/aging/`
- `/api/reports/advance-credits/`
- `/api/reports/adjustments/`
- `/api/reports/late-fees/`
- `/api/reports/ledger/`
- `/api/reports/reconciliation/`

The API uses the existing workspace permission boundary, deterministic validation, Decimal-safe money serialization, and GET-only read behavior.

## Locked Financial Semantics

The reporting layer preserves the canonical position:

`gross_receivable + debit_adjustments - reducing_adjustments + late_fees = adjusted_receivable`

`settlement = payment_allocations + advance_credit_applications`

`outstanding = max(adjusted_receivable - settlement, 0)`

Recorded payments remain historical cash receipts. Allocation and advance-credit application are settlement movements, not additional cash receipts. Only successful refunds reduce recognized cash. Adjustments and late fees affect receivables and do not mutate historical payment facts. Final settlement does not fabricate a payment or refund.

## Security and Integrity

- Existing workspace authorization remains the access boundary.
- PostgreSQL RLS remains enabled and forced for protected financial data.
- Reporting queries are workspace-scoped.
- Reporting endpoints do not accept an alternate workspace identifier to bypass request workspace context.
- No reporting operation creates, updates, deletes, allocates, settles, refunds, repairs, or replays financial records.
- Prior P0.1–P0.11 contracts remain protected.

## Deferred Beyond P0.12

The following remain intentionally outside this phase:

- accounting / GL;
- GST and tax filing;
- gateway and bank reconciliation;
- automatic reconciliation repair/replay;
- true historical balance reconstruction;
- exports;
- materialized reporting infrastructure unless justified by scale;
- forecasting;
- analytics redesign;
- AI financial intelligence.

## Closure

P0.12 is now a stable read-only financial reporting boundary over the completed canonical financial state and ledger foundation. Future phases may consume these reporting contracts, but must not silently turn reporting into a mutation authority or change the locked financial semantics.
