# P0.12-C — Period & Aging Reports

**Status:** P0.12-C HARDENED — pending exact-head CI lock

## Purpose

P0.12-C extends the read-only P0.12 reporting boundary with deterministic period collection reporting and invoice aging projections. It does not create or mutate financial state.

## Locked Contract

### 1. Read-only reporting

Period and aging reports are projections only. They MUST NOT mutate Invoice, Payment, PaymentAllocation, AdvanceCredit, FinancialAdjustment, LateFee, Refund, FinalSettlement, Occupancy, or FinancialLedgerEntry records.

### 2. Collection period semantics

`collection_period_report(workspace, start, end)` uses an inclusive calendar-date range.

- Payment cash is recognized using `Payment.payment_date`.
- Refund cash reduction is recognized only for ledger events with `event_type=refund_succeeded`.
- Successful refund timing uses `FinancialLedgerEntry.occurred_at` calendar date.
- Failed/processing/requested refunds do not reduce collections.
- Reversed ranges are invalid.
- Workspace scope is mandatory at the query boundary.

### 3. Aging semantics

`aging_report(workspace, as_of)` classifies the **current canonical outstanding balance** of each workspace invoice by its due date relative to `as_of`.

Buckets are locked as:

- `current`: due today or in the future
- `1_30`: 1–30 days overdue
- `31_60`: 31–60 days overdue
- `61_90`: 61–90 days overdue
- `90_plus`: more than 90 days overdue

Invoices with canonical outstanding `<= 0` are excluded.

The report does not rewind or reconstruct historical financial state. It uses the canonical financial position available at query time with the explicitly supplied `as_of` classification date.

### 4. Canonical financial position

Aging uses `calculate_invoice_financial_position()` as the sole balance calculation boundary. Therefore payment allocations, advance-credit applications, debit/reducing adjustments, and late fees retain the existing P0 financial semantics.

### 5. Precision and determinism

Money remains `Decimal` with two-decimal financial precision. Invoice ordering is deterministic by `due_date, pk`; ledger activity ordering is deterministic by `occurred_at, pk`.

### 6. Validation

Date inputs accept `date` and `datetime` values and are normalized to calendar dates where the period contract requires dates. Unsupported values raise `ValidationError` rather than being silently coerced.

### 7. Workspace isolation

All report queries are workspace-scoped. Existing PostgreSQL RLS, FORCE RLS, and application-level authorization boundaries remain unchanged.

## Hardening Coverage

Dedicated regression coverage verifies:

- all aging bucket boundaries;
- future and due-today invoices remaining Current;
- fully settled invoices excluded;
- canonical partial-payment + adjustment + late-fee outstanding;
- inclusive period start/end boundaries;
- successful versus failed refund semantics;
- payment-date period semantics;
- workspace isolation for period reporting;
- reversed-period rejection and invalid `as_of` rejection;
- read-only behavior.

## Non-goals

P0.12-C does not implement historical balance reconstruction, accounting/GL, GST reporting, gateway/bank reconciliation, materialized reporting tables, forecasting, or automated financial repair.

## Acceptance

P0.12-C is considered complete only after the dedicated regression suite and full Django CI pass on the exact branch head. The branch is then locked without changing P0.1–P0.12-B contracts.
